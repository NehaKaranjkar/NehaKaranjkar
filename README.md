### Hi, I'm Neha Karanjkar

Assistant Professor of Computer Science and Engineering at [IIT Goa](https://www.iitgoa.ac.in/).
I work on parallel and hybrid simulation, performance modeling of computer systems, and simulation-driven design of discrete-event systems.
I build open-source frameworks that put these ideas into practice.

**Research focus**
- Synchronization of multi-agent (LLM/AI) systems: conservative-to-optimistic execution, speculation and rollback
- Parallel and hybrid (discrete-event + continuous) simulation
- Cycle-level performance models of processors, accelerators and systems-on-chip
- Digital twins and supply chains: automated model generation, learned surrogates (GNNs)
